# PEP 600 authority summary

Primary source: https://peps.python.org/pep-0600/

- Status: Final.
- Header: replaces PEPs 513, 571, and 599.
- The replacement is a perennial, glibc-versioned policy for future tags.
- The accepted “Legacy manylinux tags” section preserves the old names as
  aliases: `manylinux1` for glibc 2.5 on x86, `manylinux2010` for glibc 2.12 on
  x86, and `manylinux2014` for glibc 2.17 on its supported architectures.

Thus “replaces” is not authority to delete every behavior established by the
earlier PEPs.
