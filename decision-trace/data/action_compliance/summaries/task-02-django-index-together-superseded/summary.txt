* **Deprecation and Removal**: `Meta.index_together` and `AlterIndexTogether` were deprecated in Django 4.2 (ticket #27236, commit `a6385b382e`) in favor of `Meta.indexes = [models.Index(...)]`, raising `RemovedInDjango51Warning` (Source: artifact_001, artifact_002, artifact_003). `Meta.index_together` was permanently removed in Django 5.1 (commit `2abf417c81`) (Source: artifact_003).
